Child Safety Policy

Version 1.0 — Last updated 2026-11-22

1. Who is responsible

Unmelt is operated by Mamonta Dış Ticaret A.Ş. (the "operator", "we", "us"). The operator is responsible for applying this policy and for child safety reports. This policy applies to the Unmelt app on the App Store and Google Play and to everyone who uses it. Unmelt complies with applicable child safety laws.

2. Our stance

There is no place on Unmelt for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Creating, uploading, storing, sharing or requesting child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is strictly prohibited. Any content or behaviour that sexualises a child, encourages abuse or aims to harm a child is also prohibited.

3. How we prevent it

  • Stories are generated under age-appropriate rules.
  • A story sent to the library is screened automatically and only published after our team reviews it.
  • Every story can be reported from within the app; our team reviews reports.

4. When suspected content is found

  • Suspected content is removed immediately and preserved as evidence to the extent the law allows and requires.
  • Suspected child sexual abuse material is reported to the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC, CyberTipline) and, where required, to the competent law enforcement authorities.
  • The related account is closed and is not allowed to be reopened.

5. Reporting and contact

Use the "Report" option in the app or email privacy@legal.mamonta.com. If you believe a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first.
