Delete your Unmelt account

Last updated 2026-11-22

App: Unmelt. Operator: Mamonta Dış Ticaret A.Ş., Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: privacy@legal.mamonta.com.

First: cancel your subscription in the store

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google. Cancel it in your store account settings:

  • Android: open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Unmelt → Cancel subscription.
  • iPhone or iPad: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Unmelt → Cancel Subscription.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their rules.

Option 1: delete your account in the app

  1. Open Unmelt and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile screen.
  3. If the app shows “Parents only”, answer the question and tap “Continue”.
  4. Tap “Delete my account”.
  5. The app asks “Delete your account?”. Tap “Delete my account”.
  6. The app asks “One last check: are you sure?” and lists what will be deleted. Tap “Yes, delete permanently”.

The deletion starts when you confirm. You are then signed out. You can't undo this.

Option 2: ask us by email (no app needed)

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, email privacy@legal.mamonta.com with the subject “Delete my account”. Send it from the email address on your Unmelt account, so we can find the account. If you write from another address, we will ask you to confirm the request from the account's address before we delete anything.

We delete the account and tell you by email within 30 days at the latest. The same data is deleted as when you delete in the app.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name, password (if you signed up with email) and internal user ID.
  • Your child profiles, and with them the reading days, reading and listening events, favourites, Story Radio listening history and any feelings answers from an earlier version of the app.
  • Earlier names of a child profile that we kept for search.
  • All your personal books: their text and earlier text versions, hero name and drawing, pictures, animations, narration, music and PDF files, your answers to the order questions and your change requests. Files are removed from our file storage shortly after the rest.
  • Books you sent to the library and their library copy, and share links of your books with the list of people who opened them.
  • Your remaining book credits, credit history and subscription status in our database.
  • Your narrator and background-sound choices.
  • Your consent records and your acceptance records for the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.
  • Your push token and in-app notifications.
  • Any entry in the audit log of text processed without consent.
  • On the device you use to delete: the reading counters and other data the app keeps on the device.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Store purchase event log: event ID, event type and time. Your internal user ID is removed from it. Kept for accounting, refund and fraud handling.
  • Review and moderation records about a book you published (review actions, admin audit, content reports others sent). They are no longer linked to your account.
  • Encrypted backups of our database and file storage: kept for up to 30 days, then deleted. A non-identifying encrypted deletion mark (HMAC) is kept for 60 days so that a deleted account is not restored from a backup.
  • Records we must keep by law.
  • Your subscription record held by RevenueCat, Apple and Google: kept under their own rules.
  • Error reports kept by Sentry, under our Sentry retention settings. They contain no information that identifies you.
  • Copies in your own device backups (for example iCloud or Google backups). These are in your own account and we cannot access them.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • A book: open the book and tap “Delete book”. A book that is still being made can be deleted after it is finished.
  • A child profile: open the profile and tap “Delete profile”. If the child has books, delete those books first.
  • Consent for the text you write about your child: you can withdraw it on the Profile screen.
  • The text you wrote about your child is deleted automatically within 90 days at the latest. To ask for earlier deletion, a copy of your data or any other request, email privacy@legal.mamonta.com from the email address on your account.

For full detail, see the Privacy Policy, sections 7 and 8.
