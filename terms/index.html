2. What Unmelt is

Unmelt is an app for parents and guardians. It offers a library of narrated, animated picture-book stories for children about everyday feelings, to read or play together with your child. You can also order a personal book for your child: you answer a few questions, we make a draft and a hero drawing with AI tools, you approve the draft or ask for a change, and we then make the pages, narration and music (see section 8). Personal books are for children aged 1 to 8.

This feature is not available in this version of the app. If we add it in a later version, it will work as described below and only when you ask for it.

You can send a finished personal book to the Unmelt story library; if it is published, other families can read it too. We publish a book only if you ask: nothing you make is sent to the library automatically.

A name in the story, including your child's name or the hero's name, is not removed or changed, so please read the book before you send it.

  • You can withdraw the book while it is being reviewed, and you can take it down after it is published, at any time in the app. Deleting the book or your account also removes it from the library.
  • We have no tolerance for content that breaks section 6. If a book breaks these Terms, we may decline it or take it down, and we may stop your account from sending books to the library. A book we take down cannot be sent again.
  • Anyone can report a story with the in-app "Report" option. We review reports about stories that other users can see (library stories and books shared by link) within 24 hours, and other reports within 48 hours, and we remove content that breaks these Terms.
