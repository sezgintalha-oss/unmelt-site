## 2. What Unmelt is

Unmelt is an app for parents and guardians. It offers a library of narrated,
animated picture-book stories for children about everyday feelings, to read or
play together with your child. You can also order a personal book for your
child: you answer a few questions, we make a draft and a hero drawing with AI
tools, you approve the draft or ask for a change, and we then make the pages,
narration and music (see section 8). Personal books are for children aged 1
to 8.

## 3. Not therapy or medical advice

Unmelt stories are for reading together and starting conversations. They are

about your child's health, development, safety or wellbeing, contact a doctor,
psychologist or other qualified professional. If a child is in danger, call
911 (in the US) or your local emergency number.

This feature is not available in this version of the app. If we add it in a later version, it will work as described below and only when you ask for it.

- Before it is published, the book goes through an automatic check (for

  for it, and other families read it under the usual library access rules

- You can withdraw the book while it is being reviewed, and you can take it
  down after it is published, at any time in the app. Deleting the book or
